About Us

25+ years engineering fire safety across Hyderabad

Dilip Fire Safety Engineers is a full-service fire protection company — supply, design, installation and annual maintenance, handled by one accountable team.

Our Story

Built on sites that couldn't afford to get it wrong.

Dilip Fire Safety Engineers started as a small team of fire protection engineers serving factories and warehouses around Hyderabad. Over 25+ years, that grew into a full-service company handling hydrant systems, sprinklers, fire detection & alarm, LPG gas detection, extinguisher supply, AMC and staff training — for clients ranging from single-floor commercial units to multi-block manufacturing campuses.

What hasn't changed is the standard we hold ourselves to: every hydrant valve, every detector, every extinguisher we supply has to work exactly as intended in the one moment it's actually needed. That means proper design against NBC norms, certified components, disciplined installation, and an AMC schedule clients can actually rely on — not just a certificate that says they can.

Today we support factories, warehouses, hospitals, malls, residential complexes and commercial buildings across Hyderabad, Secunderabad and wider Telangana, with a technician team available for emergency call-outs around the clock.

How We Got Here

A brief timeline.

Early Years

Founded as a small fire protection engineering team, taking on hydrant and extinguisher work for local factories and warehouses around Hyderabad.

Expansion Into Full Systems

Added sprinkler design, fire detection & alarm systems and LPG gas detection to the service list, moving from single-product supply into full-system contracting.

AMC Program Formalised

Built a dedicated AMC program with scheduled visits, signed test logs and compliance documentation — after seeing how many sites were let down by inconsistent maintenance vendors.

Today

150+ active AMC clients, a trained technician bench available for 24×7 emergency response, and a growing training program helping client staff run their own evacuation drills.
