Fire extinguishers, matched to the fire risk

Not every space needs the same extinguisher. We assess your site's fire risk — electrical panels, kitchens, flammable-liquid storage, general office space — and recommend the right mix of types and capacities, then handle supply, wall-mounting, signage and the paperwork your fire NOC will ask for.

Types we supply

  • ABC (Dry Chemical Powder) — all-purpose, for Class A, B & C fires, 2kg–9kg cylinders.
  • CO2 Type — clean agent, ideal for electrical panels and server rooms, leaves no residue.
  • Water CO2 Type — Class A fires in offices, lobbies and general areas.
  • Mechanical Foam — flammable-liquid risk areas, warehouses and fuel storage.
  • Modular / Automatic Type — ceiling-mounted, self-activating on ambient heat, for unattended electrical rooms.

Refilling & hydrostatic testing

Extinguishers lose pressure and their agents degrade over time — an unrefilled cylinder is a false sense of security. We track refill due-dates for every extinguisher we've supplied or taken over, and carry out hydrostatic pressure testing on cylinders at the intervals fire safety codes require, with a tag/certificate left on each unit showing the next due date.

Why site managers choose us

Because we don't just drop off cylinders — we also handle refilling, hydrostatic testing, wall-mount installation, signage and an AMC that keeps every unit compliant year-round, at prices that are genuinely competitive against other suppliers in Hyderabad.
