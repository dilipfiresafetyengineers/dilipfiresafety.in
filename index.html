Frequently Asked

Common questions before you call.

How much does a fire hydrant system cost in Hyderabad?

Click for more

Cost depends on building height, hydrant points, pump room specification and pipe routing. Share your site plan or built-up area with us and we'll give you a written quote after a free site visit.

Do you provide AMC for systems installed by another company?

Click for more

Yes. We take over AMC for existing hydrant, sprinkler, detection, LPG detection and extinguisher systems regardless of who installed them, after an initial inspection.

Can you help with fire NOC documentation?

Click for more

Yes. Our installations and AMC visits come with the test logs, drawings and completion certificates most Telangana fire departments ask for during NOC application and renewal.

Which fire extinguisher is suitable for my shop, office or building?

Click for more

The right fire extinguisher depends on the type of fire risk at your premises. We can assess your shop, office, warehouse, commercial building or industrial site and recommend suitable extinguishers such as ABC, CO2, DCP or other types based on the requirement.

Do you provide fire extinguisher refilling and servicing?

Click for more

Yes. We provide fire extinguisher inspection, servicing, refilling and maintenance for different types of extinguishers. Contact us with the extinguisher type and quantity for service details and pricing.

How often should fire extinguishers be inspected or serviced?

Click for more

Fire extinguishers should be checked regularly for pressure, damage, accessibility and overall condition, with servicing carried out according to the extinguisher type, applicable standards and manufacturer requirements. Our team can inspect your extinguishers and advise when servicing or refilling is required.

Do you install fire alarm and smoke detection systems?

Click for more

Yes. We undertake fire alarm and detection system projects for commercial, residential and industrial premises, including control panels, smoke and heat detectors, manual call points, hooters and related equipment based on project requirements.

Do you provide a site visit before giving a quotation?

Click for more

Yes. For projects where a site assessment is required, our team can inspect the premises, understand the fire safety requirements and prepare a quotation based on the scope of work, equipment and installation requirements.

How much does a complete fire safety system cost?

Click for more

The cost depends on the size and type of property, fire risks, required equipment and project scope. A small shop may only require basic fire safety equipment, while larger buildings may require hydrant, sprinkler, alarm and detection systems. Contact us with your requirements for a quotation.

Do you provide AMC for fire safety systems?

Click for more

Yes. We provide Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC) services for fire hydrant, sprinkler, fire alarm, detection, extinguisher and other fire safety systems. Existing systems installed by other companies can also be considered after inspection.

Do you provide fire safety services for small shops and businesses?

Click for more

Yes. We provide fire safety solutions for small shops, offices, restaurants, clinics, warehouses and other businesses. Whether you need a single fire extinguisher or a complete fire safety system, we can recommend and install the right solution based on your premises and requirements.

Do you provide fire safety services all over India?

Click for more

Yes. Dilip Fire Safety Engineers undertakes fire safety projects across India. Our team can support commercial, industrial, residential and institutional projects based on the site requirements and scope of work.

Do you install fire hydrant systems?

Click for more

Yes. We undertake complete fire hydrant system projects, including system planning, piping, hydrant points, hose reels, valves, pumps, accessories, installation, testing and commissioning according to project requirements.

Do you handle hydrant, fire alarm and other fire safety projects across India?

Click for more

Yes. We undertake fire hydrant, sprinkler, fire alarm, fire detection, LPG detection, fire extinguisher and other fire safety projects across India. Our services are not limited to Hyderabad and Telangana; we can execute projects nationwide based on project requirements.

Can I contact you for only one fire extinguisher?

Click for more

Yes. You can contact us for small requirements as well as complete fire safety projects. Whether you need a single fire extinguisher for a shop or a complete system for a commercial or industrial facility, our team can assist you.
