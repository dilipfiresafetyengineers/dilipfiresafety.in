Why AMC matters more than the install

A fire safety system that isn't maintained degrades quietly — a pressure drop here, a dead detector there — until the one time it's actually needed. Most sites we take over from other vendors weren't badly installed; they were poorly maintained. Our AMC is built to fix that: scheduled visits, real test logs, and a technician who shows up when they say they will.

What's covered

  • Fire hydrant systems — pump testing, valve checks, pressure testing
  • Sprinkler systems — flow testing, alarm valve checks, head inspection
  • Fire detection & alarm — detector testing, panel diagnostics, battery checks
  • LPG gas detection — sensor calibration, solenoid valve testing
  • Fire extinguishers — refilling, hydrostatic testing, mounting checks

Visit frequency

We offer quarterly, half-yearly and annual AMC plans depending on your building's risk profile and local compliance requirements — higher-occupancy and industrial sites typically benefit from quarterly visits, while smaller commercial spaces are often well-served by half-yearly.

What you get after every visit

A signed test log, a list of any components that need replacement, and — where relevant — a compliance certificate you can use directly for your fire NOC renewal. Priority call-out support is included for all active AMC clients.

Taking over from another vendor

We regularly onboard sites whose previous AMC vendor stopped showing up. We start with a full inspection against current code, flag anything that needs urgent attention, and bring the system onto a proper schedule from there.
