Why training matters as much as equipment

A building can have every hydrant, detector and extinguisher installed correctly and still lose critical minutes in a real fire if staff don't know the evacuation route, how to raise the alarm, or how to use a fire extinguisher. Training closes that gap — and most fire NOC renewals expect evidence of it.

Programs we run

  • Fire Evacuation Drills — full-building drills with timed assembly-point reporting and a written debrief.
  • Extinguisher Usage Training — hands-on practice on ABC, CO2 and foam extinguishers using live-fire training props, so it's muscle memory, not a slideshow.
  • Fire Warden Training — role-specific training for designated fire wardens covering alarm response, headcounts and escalation to emergency services.
  • Emergency Response Training — classroom plus practical session on alarm protocols, safe assembly, and coordination with local fire services.

Scheduled around your operations

Sessions are scheduled to suit your building's operating hours — before shift start, during a low-occupancy window, or split across multiple sessions so operations aren't disrupted.

What you get afterward

A written debrief report and attendance record for each session — useful both internally and as part of your fire safety compliance documentation.
