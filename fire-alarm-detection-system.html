Addressable vs. conventional — which does your site need?

Conventional systems wire detectors into zones and tell you which zone triggered an alarm. Addressable systems report each device individually, so you know the exact detector — useful for larger or multi-floor buildings where a faster, more precise response matters. We'll recommend the right fit based on your building size and budget.

What's included

  • Addressable or conventional smoke and heat detectors
  • Manual call points at exits and stairwells
  • Hooters / sounders with strobe for audible-visual alerts
  • Fire Alarm Control Panel (FACP) sized to your zone count
  • Full zone mapping and wiring documentation
  • Commissioning, testing and staff walkthrough of panel operation

Integrating with existing systems

If you already have a fire alarm panel from another vendor, we can extend it, replace failing detectors, or take over its maintenance under AMC — you don't need to rip out a working system to get better service.

Testing that doesn't disrupt your building

Routine detector testing is scheduled to avoid disrupting occupied spaces, with test results logged against each device for your compliance records.
