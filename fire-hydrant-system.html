What a hydrant system needs to get right

A fire hydrant system is only as good as its weakest link — pump capacity, pipe sizing, landing valve placement and available water storage all have to work together. We design each system around your building's height, occupancy and hazard class, then handle supply, installation, flushing and commissioning end to end.

What's included

  • Site survey and hydraulic design sized to your building
  • Single/double-headed landing valves, internal and external hydrant points
  • Hose reel drums, RRL hoses, branch pipes and nozzles
  • Hydrant pump sets — electric, diesel standby and jockey pump
  • Underground or terrace fire water storage tanks sized to NBC norms
  • Flushing, flow-testing and formal commissioning with test records

Taking over an existing hydrant system

If your building already has a hydrant system installed by another contractor, we can inspect it, flag any gaps against current code, and either repair specific components or bring the whole system onto our AMC — so it's tested and documented going forward instead of being an unknown quantity.

Documentation for your fire NOC

Every installation and AMC visit comes with test logs, drawings and completion certificates — the paperwork Telangana fire departments typically ask for during NOC application and renewal.
